Changelog

v0.17.1
  • Fixed: monsters, arrows, explosions and falls now really hurt you in survival
  • Fixed: your own character shows in third-person views (F5)
  • Fixed: a blaster's fuse resets when you get away from it
  • Faster block lookups; multiplayer library no longer delays start-up
  • Source split into separate files for GitHub
v0.17.0
  • Pirate Cove: a big fortified hideout on the beach - stone tower with a cellar, tavern, barracks, armory, captain's cabin, prison, treasure vault, a pier and a pirate ship (find it with /locate pirate_cove tp)
  • New system for big multi-room structures: rooms are joined by corridors in a new random layout every time ; the cove is the first one
  • Wide sandy beaches with palm trees on new worlds (old worlds keep their terrain; use /structure pirate_cove anywhere)
  • New blocks: palm log, palm leaves, sandstone, dark planks, stone bricks, gold block, barrel (all craftable or lootable)
v0.16.0
  • Textures & skin editor (main menu and pause menu)
  • Texture pack: repaint any game texture; only you see it; export/import packs (Texture Gallery format)
  • Custom skins: paint your player; everyone in the room sees it; import/export skins
v0.15.0
  • /border: add a wall of any radius to any world, shrink it over time, or remove it
  • Arena: the zone starts shrinking after 5 minutes, with on-screen warnings
  • /title: big on-screen titles (shown to everyone when hosting)
  • Mob textures are never stretched (each face shows its real pixel size); removed 13 unused textures
v0.14.0
  • Name tooltips when hovering items and slots
  • Armour shows on your character (third person) and on monsters, which sometimes spawn wearing it
  • Zombies and skeletons can carry swords, axes, bows or crossbows
  • Arena: 24 loot chests spread across the arena
  • Third person: the head follows where you look
  • Transparent pixels on mob models (skeleton ribs); spider legs fan out properly; villagers have normal arms
v0.13.0
  • Crafting stations: crafting table, smithing table (tools + armour), furnace (smelting, cooking), loom (dyes, wool), fletching table (bows, arrows)
  • Armour (leather, iron, gold, diamond) with slots and an armour bar
  • Bows (hold to draw), crossbows (load, then fire), arrows
  • New mobs: cow, chicken, skeleton (shoots), blaster (explodes); zombies attack villagers and hold their arms forward
  • Villages: houses, a smithy, a well, lamps, crafting tables and gravel paths
  • Every screen has a ✕ in the corner; the key that opens a screen (E, M) also closes it
  • Chests show only the chest and your inventory; the crafting list can be collapsed
  • Fullscreen button; proper torch icons; cooked food
v0.12.0 — now called Cubora
  • Structure system (STRUCTURES list) with generated houses: chest with loot and a villager who trades for emeralds
  • Chests (craft from 8 planks; shift-click moves stacks)
  • Seagrass, kelp and fish in oceans, rivers and lakes
  • Block outline follows the block's real shape (torches, plants, chests); aiming hits the shape
  • Debug info hidden; F3 shows it
  • Scrollable command suggestions (click to pick), close button on chat
  • Stars over the whole sky; smoother clouds that fade at the edge
  • /structure, /locate house
v0.11.0
  • World options when creating (type, game mode, start time, animals, monsters, day cycle, seasons, join rank) and an Edit button (also in the pause menu)
  • World types: Normal, Amplified, Islands, Superflat, Arena (walled arena + centre loot)
  • Coloured lighting: sky light + torches you can dye (7 colours); caves and nights are dark
  • Block-break particles, 10-stage crack animation
  • First-person hand/held item with bob and swing; F5 third person (back/front); F2 screenshots; F1 hide HUD
  • Health, fall damage, food, death + respawn; zombies and spiders at night (burn in daylight)
  • /xray, /heal, /hurt, /rank, /ranks, /joinrank, /kick
  • Ranks: guest, player, admin, host
v0.10.0
  • Command autocomplete (Tab) with usage hints; many new commands (/help lists them)
  • Flowers and tall grass, dyes, 7 coloured wools, sticks, ingots
  • Tools from wood, stone, iron, gold, diamond; timed mining in survival, ore tiers, sword damage
  • Crafting list in the inventory
  • Clouds
  • Pig, sheep and player textures sized to their model (1 pixel = 1/16 block)
  • Spawn always on flat open grass; you get pushed out if stuck in blocks
v0.9.0
  • Zones: deep ocean, shallow ocean, beach, river, lake, fields, forest, mountains, underground (shown in the HUD, /zone)
  • Day/night cycle with sky gradient, sunrise/sunset, sun, moon and stars (/time)
  • Four seasons that change each zone differently (/season)
  • Ores (coal, iron, gold, diamond), gravel; spaghetti, noodle and cheese caves
  • Textured pig, sheep and Humanoid player models
  • Dropped blocks are spinning mini blocks
  • Spawn is always on grass in the open (/spawn)
v0.8.0
  • Edited chunks re-render instantly (no seeing through walls when mining fast); closer camera near plane for wide FOV
  • Settings remembered on the device; first launch picks settings for your device
  • Mobile touch controls (joystick, look, tap/hold, buttons)
  • Inventory (E): 36 slots, stacks of 64; survival and creative (/gamemode)
  • Entities: pigs and sheep that wander, jump, panic and drop loot; dropped items you pick up (Q drops)
  • Chat (T) with commands (/help)
v0.7.0
  • Render distance up to 32 chunks
  • Far terrain is now a slider (off, ×1.5 to ×8) using a distance quadtree: detail halves each step further away
  • Far terrain shows real trees nearby and forests further out
  • Forests: tree density varies by area; new birch and fir trees (with their logs and leaves in the hotbar)
  • Leaves are see-through
  • Static meshes skip per-frame matrix updates
v0.6.0
  • Block update system: only blocks near edits or told by the generator get checked
  • Flowing water with levels; springs flow down the mountain and settle
  • Climb out of water: jump at the surface next to a block for a bigger hop
  • Better underwater look (tint, vignette, denser blue fog)
  • Round minimap
  • Fixed see-through gaps between far terrain and loaded terrain
v0.5.1
  • Distance haze: far terrain stays visible but soft, fading fully only at the very edge
  • No see-through holes at the edge of loaded terrain: stand-in stone (or dirt/grass) is shown until the real chunk loads
v0.5.0
  • Smooth far-terrain handover: low-detail land stays until the real chunks there are built (no gaps while loading)
  • Far terrain on/off in Settings
  • World map of explored terrain (M key / pause menu), saved in the world, drag + zoom
  • Minimap (can be turned off in Settings)
v0.4.0
  • Infinite world height: 3D chunks load in a sphere around you (up and down too)
  • Spherical fog in every direction
  • Far terrain: simplified, lower-detail landscape out to 2× render distance
  • Mountains with bare stone and snowy peaks, new Snow block
  • Water springs on mountains that are generated already flowed down as streams and waterfalls
v0.3.0
  • Smooth ambient occlusion (toggle in Settings)
  • Sprint key (hold to run faster, with FOV kick)
  • Rebindable controls in Settings
  • Pause menu shows the world blurred behind it, with a Settings button
  • Render distance up to 16 chunks
v0.2.0
  • Texture system: blocks use 16x16 textures stored as code (palette + pixel rows), atlas built at startup
  • Textures for all blocks, hotbar shows block textures; textures have width and height (rectangular allowed)
v0.1.0
  • Voxel world: grass, dirt, stone, sand, water, trees, caves
  • Break / place blocks, swimming, double-tap Space to fly
  • Saved worlds (browser storage)
  • P2P multiplayer (PeerJS): host with room code, private or public, join by code or list
  • Host-authoritative block edits, shared seed, player avatars
